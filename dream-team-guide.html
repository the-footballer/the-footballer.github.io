How to build a 4-3-3 dream team that pays you every day

How dream team points work, why every position matters, the best way to pick your eleven, and how to turn it into free coins daily.

By The Footballer team📅 18 October 2026⏱️ 3 min read
⭐

Your dream team is the most underrated feature in The Footballer. Set it up once and it earns you points and coins every single day, even on days you only open the site for a minute. Here's how it works and how to build a good one.

How dream team points work

You pick 11 players you own, one for every position of a 4-3-3. Each day, every player in your team scores between their rating minus 5 and their rating plus 5, picked at random. The random number is the same for everyone that day, so two fans with the same player get the same points.

  • A player rated 90 scores between 85 and 95 points a day.
  • Eleven players rated around 85 add up to roughly 935 points a day.
  • You get coins worth half of your team's points each day, so that team pays about 467 coins daily.
Tip: changes to your team count from the next day, so pick your team early. If you build it today, it starts earning straight away the first time.

The eleven positions

The 4-3-3 has one slot for each of these positions, and each player has to play in their own position:

LinePositionsWhat the role means
GoalGKGoalkeeper
DefenceRB · CB · CB · LBFull-backs on the sides, two centre-backs in the middle
MidfieldCDM · CM · CAMA holding midfielder, a central midfielder and an attacking midfielder
AttackRW · ST (or CF) · LWTwo wingers and a striker

Not sure what a CDM or a false nine is? Our football glossary explains every position and term.

How to pick the best eleven

1. Rating is everything

Points come straight from ratings, so the highest-rated player you own in each position should start. A 92-rated striker beats an 88-rated one every day on average.

2. Fill every slot first

An empty position means no points from it. Even a 70-rated goalkeeper is better than none, so fill all eleven, then upgrade.

3. Upgrade the weakest position

Look for your lowest-rated starter and target that position. Goalkeepers and full-backs are often the weak spots, because packs give more attackers and midfielders.

4. Use club and Icons packs for gaps

Need a top centre-back? Club packs only contain players from one club, so you can target a team known for great defenders. Legends like Paolo Maldini and Franz Beckenbauer come in the Icons pack. Read more in our pack guide.

A sample upgrade path

  1. Week 1: fill all 11 slots with whatever you have, even if some players are rated in the 60s and 70s.
  2. Week 2: use your daily dream team coins plus daily rewards on Rare packs to replace your weakest three players.
  3. Week 3 and beyond: trade doubles in the card exchange for higher rarities, then chase Epic and Legendary players during half-price Admin Abuse.

Dream team leaderboard

Your total dream team points add up over time, and the best managers appear on the dream team leaderboard in the Gallery. The earlier you start, the bigger your total grows.
